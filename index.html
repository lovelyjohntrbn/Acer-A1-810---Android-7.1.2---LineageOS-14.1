Small steps.
Real progress.

These features were tested on one tablet. Results may differ depending on the device and its firmware.

01 / GRAPHICS

Hardware acceleration

PowerVR acceleration works; a game downloaded from the Play Store ran smoothly on the test tablet.

02 / CONNECTIVITY

Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth

Wi‑Fi connected to the internet, and Bluetooth file transfer worked after a reboot.

03 / AUDIO

Speaker and microphone

Audio playback and an intelligible voice recording were tested.

04 / INPUT

Touch and motion

Touch and screen rotation work; the motion sensor was tested in a game.

05 / GOOGLE

Services and Play Store

On the test tablet, Google sign-in and downloading an app from the Play Store worked.

06 / IN PROGRESS

Camera

The camera is not working yet. VPN has not been tested, and a portable installer is still being prepared.

Before you begin

Install with care.

There is no public release yet. The current guide describes restoring a private image to the same tablet; the restore has not been tested.

Read the full guide ↗
  1. 01 Verify the model and partition layout.
  2. 02 Prepare your own backup and recovery plan.
  3. 03 Check the images and their SHA-256 hashes.
  4. 04 In SP Flash Tool select only boot and system.

Do not use Format or Firmware Upgrade. Never transfer device-specific NVRAM or other individual partitions between devices.
